Welcome to the IEEE VTS Motor Vehicles Challenge 2021 !

To develop your energy management strategy (EMS):
- Open Simulink file "VTS_challenge_2021.slx" (it is in R2015a version)
- Develop your EMS in the Energy Management Strategy subsystem (the dark blue block)
- Declare your own variables in the script "Strategy.m" in the folder "EMS" and must use the syntax "EMS.your_variable_name"
- You can add your additional data, functions, or any other files to the folder "EMS".

To run the model:
- Open "Run.m"
- Choose the driving cycle you want to test
- Run the script

A report in html format will be automatically generated and stored in the folder "Results".

All the data are in the folder "Data". More detailed documents are in the folder "Documents".

Please save your Simulink file in R2019b or earlier version. Send your program to vtschallenge2021@usherbrooke.ca with the "First Name Name IEEE challenge". Please send a "Read me" for your specific program. Make sure that your name and your affiliation are informed in the email.

Have fun !
